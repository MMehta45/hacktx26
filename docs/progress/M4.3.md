# Build 04.3 — Actionable plans, transit economics, and reconciliation

Date: 2026-10-25

## Implemented

- Added structured per-plan resources (`external_link`, `transit_route`, and
  `outreach_draft`) associated with action and evidence IDs. Unrelated Explore evidence is
  not copied into a plan.
- Added dated timeline events from scenario decisions, recovered shifts, expenses, payroll,
  bills, and repair payment events.
- Added reconciliation fields for direct versus indirect recovered shifts, remaining missed
  shifts, payday differences, direct/indirect/total income preserved, worst gap date, and
  bill-by-bill unpaid outcomes.
- Added usable copy feedback for each outreach draft. Clipboard success is shown only after
  the promise resolves; failures show a manual-copy fallback.
- Research leads now retain their specific missing requirements in the recovery response,
  instead of the generic “needs confirmation” message.

## Financial boundary

The existing deterministic simulator remains authoritative. Transit effects continue to charge
provider-observed fares on travel dates and preserve wages on their existing Friday paydays;
the plan response now exposes those resulting expenses/payday deltas when a route qualifies.
Reviews never become a mechanic quote, and a draft/conditional resource never becomes an
approved cash-flow event.

  comprehensive formula audit remain future work.
