# M5 — Financial Integrity & Recovery Ranking

## Implemented

- Added a reconciled outstanding-liability ledger with missed-at-due-date amount, later partial payments, settlement date, fees/credits, provenance, and ending balance.
- Split scheduled-obligation balance, projected available cash, and outstanding bills. Added first shortfall date, peak-gap date, peak/ending outstanding essentials, and simplified net cash after liabilities.
- Added wage accounting for earned take-home, payroll received, pay received for in-horizon shifts, and earned-but-unpaid amounts.
- Added separate historical variable-spending estimates and scheduled-only, essential-living, and full-behavioral forecast variants without duplicating scheduled bills. Gas estimates exclude modeled vehicle-outage days.
- Updated repair allocation to reserve the quote when paying it protects future work, while retaining arrears as liabilities for later settlement.
- Re-ranked plans using financial impact, liabilities, net cash, actual costs, feasibility, and unresolved-condition penalties. Updated Plan wording and metrics.
- Bumped browser recovery cache identity to `finance-v3`.

## Verification

- Backend: 90 tests passed; Ruff passed.
- Frontend: 12 tests passed; typecheck, lint, and production build passed.
- Added regression coverage for cash/liability conservation, later arrears settlement, raw-versus-liquid cash, wage timing, and variable-expense forecast variants.

## Limitations

- Variable living costs are separate historical estimates, not dated contractual charges in the core ledger.
- Repair prioritization is a deterministic scenario policy, not personalized financial advice.
