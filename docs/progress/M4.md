# Milestone 4 — Interactive recovery and optimization

Date: 2026-10-25

## Implemented

- Added versioned in-memory recovery sessions, typed answers, rich requirement states,
  answer revision, impact-aware follow-up questions, and opaque browser session restore.
- Added copy-only remote-work and rent-extension drafts. Possible remote work keeps manager
  approval pending; only explicit user confirmation changes that state.
- Added typed effects for exact-shift preservation, dated costs, bill timing/reduction,
  assistance, alternative repair quotes, borrowing/repayment, and makeup hours.
- Integrated all effects into the Build 03.5 derived shift, following-Friday payroll,
  repair-affordability, and constrained bill-allocation engine. Nessie history is read-only.
- Added deterministic one-to-three-action enumeration (maximum 92 combinations for eight
  candidates), pruning, lexicographic ranking, comparison deltas, explanations, and
  diversity selection for up to three plans.
- Expanded SerpApi normalization with transit origin/destination, times, duration, routes,
  stops, transfers, walking, fare provenance/certainty, and legs. Targeted research has
  separate per-round/session budgets and caching.
- Corrected mechanic filtering so only actual positive pricing-review excerpts can support
  an affordability recommendation. Ratings or generic reviews never supply a quote.
- Replaced the Stage 04 preview with questions, recorded-answer revision, drafts, requirement
  confirmation, multiple plans, financial/cascade comparisons, dated cash checkpoints,
  source links, next steps, and targeted research controls.

## Deterministic fixture result

Using the default $310 opening balance, $20 hourly wage, $400 quote, October 26 breakdown,
30-day fixture bills, and no actions, the simulator produced:

- $245.00 unfunded essential obligations
- $260.00 peak funding gap and −$260.00 raw ending balance
- no affordable repair date within the horizon
- 21 projected missed shifts

After explicit confirmation that remote work is possible, equipment exists, and the
manager approved the first five affected dates, the top remote-work plan produced:

- $245.00 unfunded essential obligations
- $245.00 peak funding gap and $620.00 raw ending balance
- repair funded November 6 under the unchanged bill-protective policy
- 16 total recovered shifts (five directly preserved and eleven restored through the
  earlier repair feedback loop)
- $1,280.00 total take-home preserved across affected paydays

Adding the confirmed $15 streaming cancellation raised raw ending balance to $635.00 but
did not improve the essential-bill or peak-gap criteria, so the lower-complexity remote-only
plan ranked first. These are conditional/fixture projections, not bank transactions.

## Known limitations

- Recovery sessions and targeted-query cache are in-memory and clear on backend restart.
- The prototype asks a curated high-impact question set; Gemini does not write arbitrary
  executable effects.
- External approval is user-reported, not independently verified. The app does not send
  drafts, contact providers, apply for aid, book repairs, or borrow money.
- Transit action compilation requires fares on both legs; unsupported fare products or
  transfer caps stay unknown rather than being inferred.
- Mechanic reviews prioritize whom to contact but never estimate Maya's repair price.
- Live provider availability and current route results were not rechecked in this milestone.
